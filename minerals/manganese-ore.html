Geological Overview & Extraction

Mineral Concession & Provenance

Balochistan’s ophiolitic and volcano-sedimentary belts in Lasbela and Khuzdar host extensive manganese mineral deposits dominated by pyrolusite ($MnO_2$) and psilomelane. Balochistan Minerals supplies lumpy metallurgical manganese ore specifically blended and screened for silicomanganese (SiMn) and ferromanganese (FeMn) production, agricultural micronutrients, and dry-cell battery manufacturing.

Commercial Applications

Industrial Off-Take & Usability

Silicomanganese & Ferromanganese

Crucial deoxidizer and alloying element in carbon steel, increasing tensile strength, toughness, and hardenability.

Electrolytic Manganese Dioxide (EMD)

High-purity chemical grade ore utilized in cathode formulations for alkaline and lithium-ion batteries.

Agricultural Micronutrient Fertilizers

Manganese sulfate precursor correcting soil mineral deficiencies in intensive farming.

Ceramics & Brick Colorants

Finely milled pyrolusite powder producing rich brown, black, and purplish pigments in architectural masonry.
