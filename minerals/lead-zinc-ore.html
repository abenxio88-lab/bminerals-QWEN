Mineral Concession & Provenance

The Duddar deposit in Lasbela District represents one of the largest sedimentary exhalative (SEDEX) lead-zinc deposits in South Asia. Balochistan Minerals coordinates off-take and export of high-grade sulfide flotation concentrates. Our zinc concentrates feature high grade ($55% ext{--}62% ext{Zn}$) with low iron and silica penalties, while our lead concentrates deliver valuable silver credits for international custom smelters.

Commercial Applications

Industrial Off-Take & Usability

Zinc Smelting & Galvanizing

Primary feedstock for electrolytic zinc refineries producing SHG (Special High Grade 99.995%) zinc for steel galvanizing.

Lead Smelting & Battery Grids

High-purity lead concentrate feed for secondary smelters supplying lead-acid automotive and energy storage batteries.

Zinc Die-Casting & Alloys

Raw material for Zamak die-casting alloys used across automotive, electronics, and hardware manufacturing.

Zinc Oxide & Chemical Pigments

Chemical-grade feed for rubber vulcanization, pharmaceutical ointments, and ceramic glaze manufacturing.

Logistics Flow

Mine-to-Port Supply Chain

Integrated handling and transport protocols guarantee that cargo arrives without contamination, moisture elevation, or grain degradation.

Modern Flotation Plant
Underground extracted ore is processed through multi-stage selective froth flotation circuits producing separate Zn and Pb filter cakes.
TML Moisture Control
Carefully dried to ensure moisture content remains well below the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML) to eliminate liquefaction risks at sea.
Customs & Port Staging
Transported in lined 20ft container boxes directly to Port Qasim container terminals with full SGS quality analysis.
