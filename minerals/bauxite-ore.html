Geological Overview & Extraction

Mineral Concession & Provenance

Balochistan’s coastal metallogenic belt hosts significant aluminous laterite and bauxite deposits in Lasbela District. Balochistan Minerals extracts low-silica refractory and metallurgical bauxite characterized by high alumina ($45% ext{--}58% ext{Al}_2 ext{O}_3$), favorable alumina-to-silica ratios ($>7:1$), and excellent thermal stability upon calcination for refractory, abrasive, and cement manufacturing.

Commercial Applications

Industrial Off-Take & Usability

Refractory Bricks & Castables

Calcined to produce high-alumina grog ($>60% ext{Al}_2 ext{O}_3$) with exceptional thermal resistance up to 1,750°C in steel ladles.

High-Alumina Cement & Building Chemistry

Crucial clinker additive generating rapid hardening and acid-resistant refractory calcium aluminate cements.

Brown Fused Alumina Abrasives

Smelted in electric arc furnaces to synthesize high-toughness synthetic corundum abrasives for cutting wheels and sandblasting.

Primary Alumina Refining

Suitable for Bayer process digestors yielding smelter-grade alumina for primary aluminum reduction cells.
