Geological Overview & Extraction

Mineral Concession & Provenance

The massive sedimentary evaporite beds of Spintangi in Sibi District contain some of the purest natural gypsum formations in the world. Balochistan Minerals operates high-volume open-pit quarries supplying heavy crystalline white gypsum rock ($>90% ext{CaSO}_4cdot 2 ext{H}_2 ext{O}$) to international cement mills, plasterboard manufacturers, and agricultural soil remediation markets.

Physical Specifications

Grain Sizing & Particle Distribution

Crushed Lumps: 10 mm – 150 mm (primary sizing for cement clinker co-grinding). Finely Ground Powder: 100 mesh and 200 mesh for agricultural gypsum soil conditioners.

Commercial Applications

Industrial Off-Take & Usability

Portland Cement Set-Retarder

Interground with clinker at 3%–5% ratio to control flash setting of tricalcium aluminate (C3A) and regulate cement hydration.

Plaster of Paris & Gypsum Wallboard

Calcined at 150°C to hemihydrate ($CaSO_4cdot 0.5H_2O$) for architectural drywall panels, ceiling cornices, and molds.

Agricultural Soil Remediation

Reclaims sodic alkaline soils by displacing sodium with calcium ions, improving soil permeability and water retention.

Water Clarification & Chemical Desulfurization

Coagulant in water treatment and agricultural compost conditioning.

Logistics Flow

Mine-to-Port Supply Chain

Integrated handling and transport protocols guarantee that cargo arrives without contamination, moisture elevation, or grain degradation.

Mechanized Quarrying
