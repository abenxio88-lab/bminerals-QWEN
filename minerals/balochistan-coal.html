Geological Overview & Extraction

Mineral Concession & Provenance

Balochistan contains over 450 million tons of proven coal reserves across the Sor Range, Degari, Mach, and Dukki coalfields. Balochistan Minerals extracts and distributes high-energy sub-bituminous to bituminous steam coal. Recognized for its exceptionally high gross calorific value (up to 7,200 kcal/kg) and low ash percentage, our coal provides intense, cost-effective thermal energy for cement clinker kilns, lime kilns, ceramic plants, and regional thermal power stations.

Commercial Applications

Industrial Off-Take & Usability

Cement Clinker Rotary Kilns

High thermal output ($>6,000 ext{kcal/kg}$) and high volatile matter ensure rapid ignition and sustained clinkering zone temperatures ($1,450^circ ext{C}$).

Steel Re-Rolling & Forging Furnaces

Low-ash coal used in coal-gasifier units producing clean synthetic fuel gas for billet heating furnaces.

Thermal Power Generation

Blended with imported Indonesian or South African coal to optimize thermal efficiency and reduce overall fuel unit costs.

Brickworks & Lime Calcination

Fast-burning fuel for continuous Hoffman and Bull trench kilns producing structural clay bricks and refractory lime.
