Energy Minerals

Coal field sourcing

Coal sourcing is field-led. GCV, ash, sulphur, moisture and fixed carbon must be confirmed through laboratory report for each lot.

Category 04

Energy & fuel minerals

Coal fields are presented as sourcing corridors, with commercial acceptance based on fresh analysis.

Quetta Coalfield

Sorange-Degari

Sorange-Degari is handled as a Quetta coalfield sourcing corridor. The correct commercial approach is to evaluate each lot by laboratory report before dispatch, especially where buyers have boiler or blending limits.

  • Material: coal field supply by lot
  • Use: fuel blending, industrial boilers and local energy demand
  • Buyer check: GCV, ash, sulphur, moisture, volatile matter and fixed carbon
Harnai Coal Belt

Khost-Shahrig-Harnai

Khost-Shahrig-Harnai coal is organized around field-led sourcing, lot inspection and transport planning. Buyers should confirm calorific value, ash and moisture against their plant requirements.

  • Material: coal from the Harnai belt
  • Use: industrial fuel and thermal applications
  • Buyer check: GCV, ash fusion behavior, sulphur, moisture and size
Duki

High-volume coal supply

Duki is one of the higher-volume Balochistan coal sourcing areas. It is suitable for buyers who need repeated lot availability, provided grade bands are locked through testing and shipment documentation.

  • Material: lot-based coal supply
  • Use: industrial fuel, blending and bulk supply programs
  • Buyer check: GCV, ash, total sulphur, moisture, fixed carbon and screening
Mach Corridor

Mach-Anjira

Mach-Anjira coal supply is treated as a corridor-based sourcing program where transport, lot consistency and lab reporting matter as much as the field name. Material is best sold with clear acceptance tolerances.

  • Material: coal from the Mach-Anjira corridor
  • Use: industrial heating, blending and local energy users
  • Buyer check: GCV, ash, sulphur, moisture, size and loading supervision
