Who We Are

Pioneering Ethical Mining
in Balochistan Since 2008

Balochistan Minerals is Balochistan's premier mineral extraction enterprise, committed to sustainable resource development, cutting-edge geological exploration, and transformative community partnerships across Pakistan's most mineral-rich province.

Our Legacy

Founded in 2008, we've grown from a single-site operation to a multi-mineral enterprise operating across three strategic districts in Balochistan. Our journey reflects an unwavering commitment to operational excellence, environmental stewardship, and socio-economic empowerment of local communities.
