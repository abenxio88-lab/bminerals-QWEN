Privacy Policy

How Balochistan Minerals collects, uses, protects, and handles information shared through our website, inquiries, and business communications.

At a glance

This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how long we keep it, and how you can contact us about privacy requests.

1. Introduction

Balochistan Minerals (Pvt) Ltd ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information in connection with our website, products, and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact us through contact forms or email
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or updates
  • Create an account on our portal
  • Make inquiries about products or services
  • Request information or documentation
  • Participate in surveys or feedback

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and message content.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referral source and exit pages
  • Device information and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send you requested information about our products and services
  • To improve and optimize our website and user experience
  • To send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • To analyze trends and monitor site usage
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activities
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • To facilitate business transactions and relationships

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: When you have explicitly consented to us processing your data
  • Contract Performance: To fulfill a contract or provide services you've requested
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Legitimate Interest: To pursue our legitimate business interests
  • Vital Interest: To protect your vital interests or those of others

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent or at your request
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. The retention period may vary depending on the context of the processing and our legal obligations. When your information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Secure password policies
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Employee confidentiality agreements

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: To request access to your personal data
  • Right of Rectification: To correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right of Erasure: To request deletion of your data (with exceptions)
  • Right to Restrict Processing: To limit how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability: To receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: To object to certain types of processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: To withdraw previously given consent

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your user experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us recognize you and remember your preferences. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites before providing your personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

12. International Data Transfers

Your personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your home country. By providing your information to us, you consent to such transfers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the revised policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our website or services after such modifications constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
