JORC Resource Statement

Document type: Technical summary for reserve, exploration, and assay review.

Scope

This statement summarizes the resource review workflow used for chromite, iron ore, barite, and copper-gold prospects.

Verification

Resource and grade claims are supported through sampling controls, third-party lab analysis, geological mapping, and competent technical review.

Buyer Use

Detailed technical material should be reviewed with qualified counterparties under appropriate due diligence procedures before relying on resource or grade assumptions.

Technical Information

The resource review process may include geological mapping, sampling plans, assay certificates, site photographs, historical production notes, and independent technical comments. Information is organized to help buyers and investors understand grade ranges, mineral continuity, extraction assumptions, and the limits of available data.

Commodity Coverage

Technical review areas may include chromite, iron ore, barite, copper-gold prospects, and selected industrial minerals. Each commodity requires a different evidence standard, so public resource summaries should be checked against the mineral setting, available laboratory results, and the intended commercial use of the material.

Disclosure Approach

This page provides a public summary only. Detailed technical schedules, reviewer notes, and supporting datasets should be reviewed through a controlled due diligence process before any investment decision, offtake agreement, or long-term supply commitment.
