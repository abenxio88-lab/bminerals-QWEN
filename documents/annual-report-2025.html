Annual Report 2025

Status: Web summary for buyer and investor review.

Operational Review

This summary describes chromite, iron ore, barite, and copper-gold workstreams with a focus on export-grade quality control, safer site operations, and disciplined logistics.

Commercial Focus

The 2025 program prioritizes verified product specifications, batch-level documentation, and direct buyer support for FOB and CIF trade flows through Karachi and Gwadar.

Governance

Management review covers HSE performance, community engagement, environmental controls, and traceable shipment documentation.

Product and Market Priorities

The reporting year emphasizes commercially useful mineral information for buyers evaluating Pakistan mineral supply. Priority product streams include chromite from Muslim Bagh-linked belts, iron ore for steel and infrastructure demand, copper-bearing material from Chaghi supply areas, and industrial minerals such as barite, gypsum, fluorite, and magnesite. Each stream is reviewed against buyer specifications, available lot size, laboratory evidence, and mine-to-port movement requirements.

Risk and Compliance Focus

Operational risk review includes source verification, transport reliability, moisture and sizing controls, safety observations, environmental obligations, and documentation readiness. The company continues to improve how certificates of analysis, photographs, shipment records, and buyer correspondence are organized so investors and procurement teams can complete due diligence with fewer delays.

Stakeholder Outlook

For the next review cycle, Balochistan Minerals plans to strengthen product pages, publish clearer compliance summaries, and improve visibility around ESG, HSE, logistics, and resource documentation. The goal is to make the website a more complete reference point for mineral buyers, investors, and technical partners.
