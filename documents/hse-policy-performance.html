HSE Policy & Performance

Coverage: Health, safety, emergency readiness, and site operating controls.

Safety System

Site teams use daily toolbox talks, equipment inspections, traffic separation, PPE controls, and incident escalation procedures.

Performance Review

Monthly HSE reviews monitor lost-time injuries, near misses, corrective actions, and contractor compliance.

Emergency Readiness

Response plans cover fire, medical evacuation, slope instability, haul-road incidents, and severe weather disruptions.

Core Policy Commitments

The HSE policy prioritizes prevention, visible supervision, practical training, safe equipment condition, and clear reporting. Workers and contractors are expected to follow site instructions, use required protective equipment, and report unsafe conditions before they become serious incidents.

Performance Indicators

Performance review tracks toolbox talks, inspection findings, near-miss reports, corrective action closure, traffic control observations, and contractor compliance. These indicators help management understand whether field controls are working across mining, loading, warehouse, and transport activities.

Continuous Improvement

Balochistan Minerals uses HSE review meetings to improve procedures, refresh training, and address recurring risks. Better safety performance also supports buyer confidence because reliable mineral supply depends on disciplined field operations and responsible workforce management.
