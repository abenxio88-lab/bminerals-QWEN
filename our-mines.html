Muslim Bagh Chromite Mine

Qila Saifullah District, Balochistan

Our flagship operation, established in 2008, sits atop one of Pakistan's most significant ophiolite-hosted chromite deposits. Using open-pit and shallow underground methods, we produce metallurgical-grade chromite ore (46-48% Cr₂O₃) for ferrochrome smelters and stainless steel manufacturers worldwide.

Khuzdar Barite Processing Facility

Khuzdar District, Central Balochistan

Established in 2014, this multi-stage processing facility produces API-standard barite (95%+ BaSO₄ purity, SG 4.20+) for the global oil and gas industry. Our 250-km proximity to both Karachi and Gwadar ports provides significant logistics advantages for export operations.

Chagai District, Western Balochistan
