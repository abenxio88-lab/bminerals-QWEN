Ethics Policy

Balochistan Minerals expects responsible conduct across sourcing, operations, logistics, supplier engagement and stakeholder communications.

Responsible Sourcing

We support transparent mineral sourcing, documented quality assurance, lawful operations and fair dealing with buyers, suppliers and communities.

Health, Safety and Environment

Operational decisions should protect worker safety, reduce avoidable environmental impact and respect local communities.
