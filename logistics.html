Warehousing

Storage that keeps each mineral lot dispatch ready

Before cargo moves to Karachi or Gwadar, warehouse handling gives buyers a cleaner chain of custody: lot separation, visual inspection, covered staging, weighing coordination, and dispatch documentation in one controlled workflow.

Stockyard control

Ore, concentrate and bagged material prepared for shipment.

Warehouse operations bridge mine production and export movement. Material can be held by mineral type, lot number, buyer requirement and dispatch priority, reducing confusion before trucking, container loading or port handoff.

01 Lot separation

Separate storage lanes for lumps, concentrate, bagged cargo and sample-ready material.

02 Quality staging

Material is held for visual checks, sampling coordination and buyer-specific packing instructions.

03 Dispatch control

Loading windows, vehicle movement and port documentation are aligned before cargo leaves the yard.
