Metallic minerals

Grades shown here are commercial ranges. Final sale terms should be tied to current lot assay, inspection, sizing and moisture.

Copper

Copper lumps from Chaghi

Copper-bearing ore is supplied as lumps for direct ore feed, blending and trading programs. Commercial terms should be set against fresh lot assay, sizing and moisture, with penalties agreed for deleterious elements where applicable.

Reference: an SGS-referenced sample from Chaghi reported 0.740% Cu in the submitted material (PKS2333078, AAS). Contract ranges vary by source block and upgrade step.

  • Typical range: assay-led (lot dependent)
  • Form: lumps (screened, buyer sizing on request)
  • Buyer checks: Cu%, moisture, silica, sulphur, impurities
  • Use: smelter feed, blending and copper ore trading
  • Documentation: assay report, weight slips, lot photos

Lead / Zinc

Base-metal ore from Chaghi

Lead-zinc material is supplied as lumps or concentrate depending on lot quality and buyer specification. Commercial evaluation should include separate Pb and Zn assays, potential Ag credits, and penalty elements agreed in advance.

For loading, we align packaging (bulk / jumbo bags / containers) to protect grade separation and minimize contamination during transit.

  • Typical range: 10% - 60% Pb/Zn (lot dependent)
  • Region: Chaghi
  • Buyer checks: Pb, Zn, Ag, moisture, impurities/penalties
  • Use: base-metal smelting and concentrate trading
  • Documentation: assay report + weight/packing list
