Metallic Minerals

Ore and concentrate supply

Detailed metallic product profiles for buyers evaluating smelter feed, steel raw material, base-metal lots and assay-backed shipment terms.

Category 01

Metallic minerals

Grades shown here are commercial ranges. Final sale terms should be tied to current lot assay, inspection, sizing and moisture.

Copper

Copper lumps from Chaghi

Copper-bearing ore is supplied as lumps for direct ore feed, blending and trading programs. Chaghi material should be handled as assay-led supply, with each lot tested before contract confirmation.

  • Grade: 2% - 10% Cu
  • Availability: 3,000 - 5,000 MT
  • Use: smelting feed, blending and copper ore trading
Chromite

Chrome lumps and concentrate

Chromite from the Kharan, Dalbandin and Muslim Bagh belts is supplied in lump and concentrate form for ferrochrome, refractory and foundry buyers.

  • Lumps: 12% - 52% Cr2O3
  • Concentrate: 32% - 52% Cr2O3
  • Buyer check: Cr2O3, Cr:Fe ratio, silica, sizing and moisture
Iron Ore

Hematite and magnetite feed

Kharan iron ore is positioned for steel feedstock, beneficiation and blending programs. The material is offered as lumps or concentrate depending on buyer specification.

  • Lumps: 40% - 58% Fe
  • Concentrate: 50% - 62% Fe
  • Use: steel feedstock, DRI blending and beneficiation
Antimony

Antimony ore and concentrate

Washuk and Zhob antimony material is presented in lumps and concentrate form. Because antimony ore varies sharply by vein and processing stage, sale terms must follow fresh assay.

  • Lumps: 4% - 58% Sb
  • Concentrate: 20% - 60% Sb
  • Use: alloys, chemicals, flame retardants and specialty metal supply
Lead / Zinc

Base-metal ore from Chaghi

Lead-zinc material is supplied as lumps or concentrate depending on lot quality. Commercial evaluation should include separate Pb and Zn assays plus silver credits and penalty elements.

  • Grade: 10% - 60% Pb/Zn
  • Region: Chaghi
  • Use: base-metal smelting and concentrate trading
