Real Impact

Transforming Communities, One Initiative at a Time

Beyond extraction, we invest in the people and places that make our operations possible.

Land Reclamation Program

Every mined area is systematically backfilled, topsoil restored, and replanted with native vegetation. We've reclaimed 120+ hectares to date—transforming extraction sites into productive grazing land and community green spaces.

120+
Hectares Restored

Water Conservation Systems

Zero-discharge closed-loop water systems recycle 95% of process water. Rainwater harvesting, sedimentation ponds, and groundwater monitoring protect local water tables for community use.

95%
Water Recycled

Healthcare & Education

On-site medical clinics serve 500+ worker families. We fund 30+ education scholarships annually for local youth and operate a mobile health camp reaching remote communities in mining districts.

30+
Scholarships/Year

Skills & Livelihood Development

Vocational training in welding, machinery operation, and geological surveying creates transferable skills. We've trained 200+ local youth in employable trades since 2018.

200+
Youth Trained
