About Balochistan Minerals

Pioneering Ethical Mining
Since 2008

For over 15 years, we've been transforming Balochistan's mineral wealth into sustainable prosperity—empowering communities, advancing industry, and setting new standards for responsible extraction.

Our Journey

From Humble Beginnings to Mining Excellence

What started as a single mining operation in Muslim Bagh has grown into a multi-mineral enterprise spanning three strategic districts across Balochistan—each site carefully managed for maximum yield with minimal environmental impact.

The Foundation: 2008-2012

Founded in 2008, Balochistan Minerals began operations in Muslim Bagh, one of Pakistan's richest chromite-bearing regions. Our founders recognized the enormous potential of Balochistan's mineral deposits and established operations with a clear mandate: extract responsibly, empower locally, and deliver globally.

First Mine: Muslim Bagh chromite operations established with 50 workers

First Certification: ISO 9001 quality management achieved

Infrastructure: Built processing plant and worker facilities

Expansion Phase: 2013-2018

Building on our success, we expanded into Khuzdar district to tap into the region's vast barite reserves—critical for oil drilling operations worldwide. This period saw our workforce triple, production capacity increase fivefold, and the establishment of our first international export partnerships with Middle Eastern and Asian markets.

Production Milestone: Reached 10,000 MT annual capacity

Global Reach: First exports to UAE, Saudi Arabia, and China

Workforce Growth: Expanded to 250+ employees

Modern Era: 2019-Present

Today, Balochistan Minerals operates as a fully integrated mining enterprise with operations across Muslim Bagh, Khuzdar, and Chagai districts. We've embraced cutting-edge geological survey technology, implemented zero-discharge water systems, and established ourselves as Pakistan's premier mineral sourcing partner—with over 50,000 MT annual capacity and growing.

Safety Record: 1,500+ days without lost-time incident

Capacity: 50,000+ MT annual production across all commodities

Global Network: Active clients across 15+ countries

15+ Years of Excellence
