The Role of High-Density Barite in Deep-Well Drilling Fluids

In high-pressure, high-temperature (HPHT) oil and gas wells, maintaining hydrostatic pressure balance is paramount to prevent formation blowouts and stabilize borehole walls. Barium sulfate (BaSO₄)—with its natural chemical inertness, high density, and low abrasiveness—is the global standard weighting material.

The Khuzdar–Gunga district in central Balochistan hosts Pakistan’s most extensive, high-purity barite formation. With certified specific gravities consistently exceeding 4.20 g/cm³ (and premium lots at 4.25+ g/cm³), Khuzdar barite effortlessly satisfies API Spec 13A requirements without requiring chemical beneficiation. Frequently Asked Questions: Barite Supply & Drilling Specs

Does Khuzdar barite meet both API Spec 13A 4.20 SG and 4.10 SG standards?

Yes. Khuzdar barite naturally yields high-purity barium sulfate (BaSO₄ 92.5%–96.5%) with typical specific gravity measured by Le Chatelier pycnometer between 4.22 and 4.28 g/cm³. This readily complies with both API Spec 13A Section 7 (4.20 SG standard) and Section 9 (4.10 SG standard) for deep-well drilling fluids.

What are the allowable limits for soluble alkaline earths (as calcium) and heavy metals?

API Spec 13A strictly caps water-soluble alkaline earths (reported as calcium) at 250 mg/kg max. Khuzdar barite consistently assays between 110 and 180 mg/kg, ensuring zero flocculation or viscosity spikes in water-based bentonite muds. Heavy metal contaminants (cadmium and mercury) test well below offshore environmental thresholds (Cd < 1.0 mg/kg, Hg < 0.2 mg/kg).

What packaging options ensure zero moisture ingress during maritime transit?

Finished 200-mesh drilling barite powder is packed in 1.5 MT heavy-duty woven polypropylene jumbo bags featuring an internal polyethylene moisture barrier liner, 4-point heavy lifting loops, and a bottom discharge chute. For bagged distribution, 50kg multi-ply moisture-proof craft bags on shrink-wrapped 2 MT pallets are supplied.

What is the transit lead time from Khuzdar mine to Jebel Ali, Dammam, and Basra?

Khuzdar is located 280 km from Karachi and 350 km from Gwadar Port via the paved N-25 highway corridor. Road transit takes under 12 hours from processing plant to port quayside. Feeder vessel transit from Karachi/Gwadar to Jebel Ali (UAE) is approximately 3 to 4 days, and to Dammam (Saudi Arabia) or Umm Qasr (Iraq) is 5 to 7 days.
