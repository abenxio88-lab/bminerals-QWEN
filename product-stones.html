Dimension Stones

Decorative blocks, slabs and tiles

Stone products are selected by block recovery, color, cracks, polish, vein pattern and finishing quality rather than chemical purity.

Category 03

Dimension & decorative stones

Suitable for flooring, cladding, feature walls, tabletops, exterior surfaces and cut-to-size interior design.

Marble

Commercial and premium marble

Marble is supplied as blocks, slabs and tiles for floors, wall cladding, staircases, counters and interior stonework. Selection depends on color consistency, vein movement, cracks, polish and slab thickness.

  • Forms: blocks, slabs, tiles and cut-to-size orders
  • Finishes: polished, honed and custom surface treatment
  • Buyer check: color lot, cracks, resin/fill level, thickness and packing
White Marble

Bright white marble selection

White marble is positioned for buyers needing cleaner backgrounds, controlled veining and premium presentation for residential, hospitality and commercial interiors. It should be selected by sample approval before bulk order.

  • Selection: bright white to off-white backgrounds
  • Use: premium floors, counters, bathrooms and wall panels
  • Buyer check: whiteness, veining, shade variation, polish and edge finishing
Iranian Marble

Persian Silk Tundra Grey

Quarried in Iran, Persian Silk Tundra Grey is a refined Persian marble with soft tonal movement and fine linear veining. Its restrained grey palette suits contemporary residential, hospitality and commercial interiors where visual continuity matters.

  • Origin: selected Iranian quarries
  • Forms: slabs, tiles and cut-to-size pieces
  • Buyer check: shade range, vein direction, polish, thickness and edge finish
Iranian Marble

Persian Silk Blocks

Sourced from Iranian quarries, Persian Silk blocks are selected for usable recovery, balanced grey tone and consistent vein movement before cutting. They suit processors producing coordinated slabs, tiles and project-sized components.

  • Origin: selected Iranian quarries
  • Form: raw blocks selected by dimensions and recovery
  • Buyer check: block dimensions, fractures, color consistency and expected yield
Iranian Marble

Pietra Grey Blocks

Quarried in Iran, Pietra Grey marble combines a deep charcoal-grey ground with crisp white veining. Blocks are selected for color depth, structural integrity and recovery for processors serving premium architectural projects.

  • Origin: selected Iranian quarries
  • Form: raw blocks for slab and tile processing
  • Buyer check: dimensions, fracture control, vein distribution and usable yield
Iranian Marble

Pietra Grey Slabs

Processed from Iranian Pietra Grey marble, these slabs offer a polished charcoal surface crossed by fine white veins. Their precise Persian character suits floors, walls, counters and statement joinery, with coordinated lots available for vein matching.

  • Origin: quarried and processed in Iran
  • Forms: polished slabs and cut-to-size panels
  • Buyer check: polish, thickness, resin treatment, vein match and packing
Iranian Marble

Silver Steam — Irani White Marble

Silver Steam is a premium Iranian white marble distinguished by its luminous white background and soft silver-grey veining. Sourced from top-grade Iranian quarries, it is supplied as blocks, slabs and tiles for high-end residential, hospitality and commercial interiors. Selection is based on color uniformity, vein pattern consistency, polish quality and crack-free surfaces.

  • Forms: blocks, slabs, tiles and cut-to-size orders
  • Finishes: mirror polished, honed and brushed surface options
  • Buyer check: whiteness, silver vein consistency, cracks, resin treatment and slab thickness
