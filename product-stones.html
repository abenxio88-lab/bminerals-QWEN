Dimension Stones

Decorative blocks, slabs and tiles

Stone products are selected by block recovery, color, cracks, polish, vein pattern and finishing quality rather than chemical purity.

Category 03

Dimension & decorative stones

Suitable for flooring, cladding, feature walls, tabletops, exterior surfaces and cut-to-size interior design.

Marble

Commercial and premium marble

Marble is supplied as blocks, slabs and tiles for floors, wall cladding, staircases, counters and interior stonework. Selection depends on color consistency, vein movement, cracks, polish and slab thickness.

  • Forms: blocks, slabs, tiles and cut-to-size orders
  • Finishes: polished, honed and custom surface treatment
  • Buyer check: color lot, cracks, resin/fill level, thickness and packing
White Marble

Bright white marble selection

White marble is positioned for buyers needing cleaner backgrounds, controlled veining and premium presentation for residential, hospitality and commercial interiors. It should be selected by sample approval before bulk order.

  • Selection: bright white to off-white backgrounds
  • Use: premium floors, counters, bathrooms and wall panels
  • Buyer check: whiteness, veining, shade variation, polish and edge finishing
Onyx

Decorative translucent stone

Onyx is supplied for feature walls, tabletops, backlit panels, decorative counters and luxury interior applications. It requires more careful handling because translucency, banding and fractures define the final commercial value.

  • Forms: slabs, tiles and decorative cut-to-size pieces
  • Use: backlit walls, feature panels and luxury interiors
  • Buyer check: translucency, pattern match, cracks, resin treatment and packing
Granite

Hard-wearing granite blocks and slabs

Granite is supplied for high-traffic surfaces, exterior cladding, counters, paving and monuments. Buyers evaluate hardness, surface finish, pattern consistency and dimensional accuracy.

  • Forms: blocks, slabs, tiles and paving sizes
  • Use: counters, exterior cladding, flooring and monuments
  • Buyer check: thickness, polish, water absorption, edge finish and packaging
