Compliance & Certifications

This page summarizes the compliance information buyers commonly request before sourcing minerals from Pakistan.

Licenses and Certifications

Operational licenses, certificates of analysis, inspection documents and shipment records are shared with qualified buyers during the quotation and due diligence process.

Quality Assurance

Product specifications are supported by batch-level documentation, third-party inspection readiness and agreed buyer requirements.

Buyer Due Diligence Pack

Qualified buyers may request company registration details, mine-source documentation, certificates of analysis, photographs of active lots, weight information, and shipment paperwork before confirming a mineral purchase. The compliance review is designed to help procurement teams verify origin, quality, and export readiness without delaying commercial discussions.

Responsible Sourcing

Balochistan Minerals aims to support responsible sourcing for chromite, iron ore, copper, barite, and stone products by documenting supplier checks, site controls, and chain-of-custody steps where available. Additional evidence can be shared under a buyer due diligence process when a transaction requires formal verification.

Supplier and Tender Inquiries

Suppliers, service providers and tender participants can contact info@balochistanminerals.com.
